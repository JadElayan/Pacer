# Pacer demo script (about 5 minutes)

**Cast:** you (narrator) · a friend as **Teacher** (laptop) · a friend as **Student** (phone or second laptop).
**Example lesson:** solving `2x + 6 = 14`. Math keeps every step easy to follow.

## Before you go on stage (2 minutes)
1. Teacher laptop: open the site, **Start Meeting** (title: "Solving Linear Equations"), **Enter classroom**.
2. Click **Small class: off** so it reads **on** (lets one student trigger an alert; a real class needs 3+ for privacy).
3. The "Local demo sync" banner should NOT be showing. If it is, sync is not connected: stop and fix before presenting.
4. Student device: scan the QR (**Invite**) or enter the 6-digit code. It should say **Connected**.
5. Teacher: Add one note ("Solve for x") so the board isn't empty. Everything else is live.

## The story

### 1. The problem (narrator, 30 seconds)
"In a live lecture, a student who zones out for 30 seconds can lose the whole thread, and raising a hand in front of the class is hard. Teachers can't see who is lost. Pacer fixes both."

### 2. The teacher writes; students follow live (45 seconds)
**Teacher:** switch to *Equation* and add `2x + 6 = 14`, then click **Start new topic** and add `2x = 8`.
**Narrator:** "As the teacher writes, every student sees it instantly. In the row above the board, the AI turns what's being taught into topic cards, like automatic lecture notes. No accounts, no setup. Students join with a QR code."

### 3. The student gets help without interrupting (90 seconds)
**Student:** tap `2x = 8`, then **Why this step?**
**Narrator:** "The AI sees the step and the surrounding lesson, not just an isolated line. Notice the label: it tells the student this is an extra explanation, not the teacher's words."
**Student:** switch **Mode** to *Guided* (steps) or *Focus* (one topic at a time). Ask for **Simplify** or **Give an example**.
**Student (optional wow):** press **Snip**, drag a box around two lines, and get a deeper explanation next to the box.
**Narrator:** "Every student can learn their own way, and the class never slows down."

### 4. The student is still confused (30 seconds)
**Student:** tap **Still confused**.
**Narrator:** "This is the key moment. The student never had to speak up."

### 4b. Check-in question and snip tools (60 seconds, optional)
**Teacher:** type "What is x?" with the answer `4` in the question bar and click **Ask class**.
**Student:** a card appears above the board. Type `4` and send: "Correct!" (not graded, only so Pacer learns the student's level). Teacher's Insights shows "1 of 1 correct".
**Student:** tap `2x + 6 = 14`, open **Tools** and pick **Graph it** (a plot where the lines cross at x = 4), **Visualize it** (balance-scale steps) or **Quiz me** (private practice question).
**Student:** open **My profile** to show the private learning profile (use **Load sample data** to show what it looks like after weeks of use).

### 5. The teacher is alerted (60 seconds)
**Teacher:** the alert appears: "1 student is still confused about `2x = 8`." Open **Insights**.
**Narrator:** "The teacher sees where the class is stuck, anonymously. Never who, only what. In a real class this appears when several students are stuck, which protects privacy."
**Teacher:** click **Revisit this**.
**Student:** their screen jumps to `2x = 8` with a banner: "Your teacher is going back over: 2x = 8".
**Student:** tap **That helped**. The teacher's unresolved count drops.
**Narrator:** "Loop closed: student asks quietly, teacher adjusts, student catches up."

### 6. Close (20 seconds)
"The classroom, in sync. The teacher keeps control, each student controls their own support, and the AI connects the two."

## If something goes wrong
- Student shows "We couldn't find meeting": wrong code, or the teacher hasn't entered the room.
- No alert on the teacher side: make sure **Small class** is on and the student tapped **Still confused** (not just *Ask*).
- AI says "Demo response": no AI key is set. Answers are prewritten for the `2x + 6 = 14` example; say so honestly.
- Shortcut: the teacher's **Add demo step** button adds the lesson one step at a time.
